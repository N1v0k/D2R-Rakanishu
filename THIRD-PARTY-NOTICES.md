## SPIRV-Tools

The SPIRV-Tools optimizer library, built into libdm.so to inline shader helper code on the Adreno 750 family. (statically linked into the released app.) The app also links it into `libdmsh_shaders.so`, the shader step of the pack build on the phone (capability trimming and validation of the translated shaders).

## Shader translator of the pack build

The app carries the shader translator that builds the shader bundles on the phone: `libdmsh_xlate.so`, the
